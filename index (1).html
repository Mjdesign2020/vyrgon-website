The problem

The fight doesn't end in the cage.

Building an MMA career takes more than performance. Fighters also need visibility, connections, and opportunities to show the world what they can do.

01

Visibility

Talented athletes can struggle to get discovered beyond their immediate circles.

02

Connections

Finding relevant brands, events, and people within the MMA ecosystem can take time and effort.

03

Opportunity

Athletes need ways to showcase their journey and connect with people who can help them move forward.

Our mission

We're building a bigger future for MMA.

VYRGON is being built to bring fighters, fans, brands, event organisers, and academies closer together. Our vision is to make MMA athletes more discoverable and help create more pathways for them to grow their careers.

More visibility.More connections.More opportunities.

For fighters

Your career deserves to be seen.

Every fighter has a story. VYRGON aims to give athletes a better way to showcase theirs, connect with the MMA community, and explore new opportunities.

  • A dedicated fighter profile.
  • A place to showcase background, discipline, and achievements.
  • Better discoverability within the MMA ecosystem.
  • Opportunities to explore relevant events and connections.
  • A pathway to potential sponsorship opportunities.
