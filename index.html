How it works

From the gym to the spotlight.

Talent is built in the gym. VYRGON is being built to help it be seen. Four simple steps.

Build your profile

Add your discipline, weight class, record, highlights and a short bio in your own voice.

Get discovered

Brands, organisers, gyms and fans search by country, weight class and discipline.

Connect

Receive enquiries inside the app. Your contact details stay hidden until you accept.

Grow your career

Show your journey, find events, and build the relationships that move your career forward.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

What is VYRGON?

VYRGON is an India-first MMA platform being built to help fighters get discovered, connect with brands, organisers and gyms, and grow their careers. It’s designed as one app for the world.

When does the app launch?

The app is in development and we haven’t announced a launch date. Join the early list and we’ll tell you first.

Is it free?

Pricing hasn’t been announced yet. Joining the early list is free and doesn’t create an account or commit you to anything.

Which countries will it work in?

We’re starting in India and designing the app for fighters everywhere, with different languages and units planned.

Who can use VYRGON?

Fighters and athletes, brands and sponsors, event organisers, gyms and coaches, and fans of combat sports.

Is VYRGON a betting platform?

No. VYRGON is not a betting or gambling platform and will not offer betting in any form.

How will my information be used?

Only to send you VYRGON updates and understand who is interested. Read the Privacy Policy for details.
